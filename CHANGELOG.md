# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Made with cstack: Nevada.** A README section and `docs/showcase/nevada.md`: a fictional sparkling iced tea built from zero in the field test, from founder interview to flat wraps to can shots, with the owner's verdicts and what cstack got wrong on the way.
- **Competitor ads before any ad** (owner's rule, field test F91). New requirement `competitor_ads` and schema `competitor-ads`: a dated scan of live ads from 5 or more rivals (link, days running, format, hook, offer, claim) ending in what is saturated and where the white space is. `ugc-style-ad` and `paid-social-cutdowns` require it, the make gate requires it of any plan whose target is an ad, a scan older than 90 days no longer counts, and each creative bet is warned until its evidence cites the scan. create-brand gains a competitor-ads step before its ad family; competitor-intel names the file.
- **No polish without a pick; story versions from the verified frame** (field test F89, F90; F48 and F52 were the first two). New gate stage `cstack flows gate <plan> --stage polish`: a plan whose human pick step comes before more generation needs the owner's winners on record (`cstack sheet import`) or a waiver (`requires: owner_pick`, with by and quote); decide and final check it too. `cstack pack check` is strict at the band edge: the raw difference decides, not the rounded one shown. New `cstack image extend <frame> --aspect 9:16 --out <png> [--fit pad|crop] [--fill edge|#rrggbb]` makes story, carousel and square versions from the checked pixels, with a sidecar naming the source frame; outpainting redrew the pack every time.
- **Trials hand brand teams the founder's reactions.** A brand team's workspace now also holds `state/feedback.jsonl`, the founder's kept and killed references, inside the brand hash; ledgers and the rest of `state/` stay out.
- **Founder first, not founder only; and downstream past the pack** (owner, field test F87, F88). creative-review ends every review by pushing: the braver version and one question each on price, claim and positioning (`references/challenge.md`); a review that only matches the owner's taste is not a pass. create-brand gains a challenge on the positioning before anything is made, and a territory cstack argues for beside the founder's on the blind sheet; after craft it goes downstream: packs from the pack spec (concept-wrap, multipacks, retail display), a photoshoot on the real packs, a launch ad family with a UGC storyboard and cutdown plan, a launch page at desktop and phone width, and a replicability trial. The decide gate warns when no challenge memo exists. Brand trials add a challenger and a decider to each team, and two scenarios: launch ads with the photoshoot behind them, and the launch page with its welcome email.
- **Real pack sizes, never invented** (owner's rule, field test F83, F86). A workspace carries the real pack as a file, `*.pack-spec.yaml` (new schema `pack-spec`): front and flat sizes in mm, seals, material, the machine repeat, the source file (dieline, print file, converter spec or a measurement) and the owner's approval; a guessed size never counts. New requirement `pack_spec`: `concept-wrap`, `packaging-system`, `shelf-test` and `3d-packshot` require it, and the make gate requires it of any plan whose target is a pack and that generates media. `cstack pack check <files> --spec <file>` measures flat artwork against the print size and a picture's pack (its box from `--box` or a judge) against the front face, 4% tolerance by default; a pack seen at an angle is unverifiable and stays illustrative; exit 1 on any of these. `cstack pack spec-from-pdf` reads a print PDF's TrimBox and flags artwork of another size as an owner decision (`artwork_mismatch`). `cstack pack list` shows each spec and whether it is approved. The spec also takes what a real flow-pack and its cartons needed in a real run: film width, panels across the web, seal style, the product inside (size ranges and how it was sized), cartons (count, arrangement, inner size, faces, status, estimated), the source file's sha256, an `estimated` flag, `immutable_traits` and `forbidden_drift`; `pack list` and `pack check` warn when the artwork's front proportion or the machine repeat disagrees with the print file and no owner decision is recorded, when the source file changed, and when sizes are estimated. `cstack mockup template can --spec` draws the can at the spec's size; `--size` says it is typical. Skills read the spec first: generate-media, mockup, asset-factory, product-fidelity, brand-import, brand-verify. `cstack trial` and `cstack pack` subcommands now read their arguments correctly from the command line.
- **Brand trials: does the brand replicate?** (owner, 2026-10-05). `cstack trial plan | run | score | import` and the `brand-trial` workflow. Agent teams get only the brand's files (`brand/`, `assets/`, `references/`, `recipes/`, `briefs/`, the config; never `state/`, `work/` or keys) and answer simulated real-world problems from `trials/scenarios/` (nine to start: retail endcap, price change, a 72-hour drop, creator kit, sold out, city poster, copycat, event merch, an investor asking for "cooler"; schema `scenario`). Every brand team starts from byte-identical brand files, recorded as one hash in the plan and checked before each workspace is made. A control team answers the same problems from the brand's name and one line. Strategist, art director, copywriter, producer, makers and reviewer run as separate agent sessions in a fresh workspace per team and scenario, resume after a usage limit, and keep every transcript. The plan prices the generation round against a floor and a hard cap (default 5 and 8 USD) and gives each workspace its share of the cap as its budget. `score` builds a blind attribution sheet mixing brand and control work and a cross-team pairs sheet; `import` reports attribution against control, cross-team agreement, the reviewer's agreement with the owner, and spend. Docs: `docs/trials.md`.
- **On-pack type checked as designed, honest billing runs, and guesses booked as guesses** (field test F76 to F80). `cstack image text --expect "<line>"` (repeatable) or `--expect-file <lines.txt>` turns the check round for designed lettering: each declared line must be read as written (one OCR slip in a long unaccented word allowed; prices, numbers and accents exact) and only missing, garbled or undeclared lettering fails; the judge engine gets the same list. `cstack spend reconcile` asks one request at a time, backs off on 429 (Retry-After, else 1, 2, 4, 8 s) and stops with the count left rather than writing error rows; a missing key or one without fal's ADMIN scope stops on the first request with the fix and writes nothing, and a run that learns nothing prints cstack's estimate as an estimate. A price typed into a request is booked with `price_source: request`, and `spend summary` says how much of the total rests on such prices. A request can name the flat artwork it `depicts`; the sidecar records it and every sheet labels the render illustrative. `concept-wrap` gains an optional scene step that uses both. Tests no longer use a field-test brand name.
- **A pack is flat artwork with real type; the model makes only the picture** (field test F73 to F75). New flow `concept-wrap`: the wrap spec at the can's real size, the layout as SVG with the brand's type, a prompt review, the picture alone from the image model, then the wrap on a can with `mockup render` and `verify`, and the owner's pick on a sheet. `cstack mockup template can --out <dir> --size standard-12oz|sleek-12oz|tall-16oz` draws a CC0 can template (front, left and back placements) and prints the flat wrap size in mm and px. The make gate warns when a plan's target is a pack (can, label, box, bottle, wrap) and its flow has an image model make it, and points at `concept-wrap`; `mood-probes` and generate-media say the same.
- **The checks pass on a Mac** (F72). Five tests failed only on macOS: update fixtures now name their branch outright, the hosts test resolves the `/var` symlink, and type qa serves a macOS system font as its web font when there is no Linux one.
- **Review the prompts before paying, keep what the territory needs, and know the real product** (field test F68 to F71, F33 to F35). `mood-probes`, `brand-hero-photo` and `grid-pick-polish` gain a prompt-review step (creative-review reads the compiled prompts against the territory and the founder's keeps and kills), and the make gate warns when a plan pays for generation with no prompt review before it. Territories can list `composite` elements (a price, a drop number, the wordmark); the decide gate refuses a plan that needs them and has no composite step, so the no-lettering rule no longer strips them. `cstack prompt compile` warns when slots contradict each other (no lettering against a printed price, close-up against wide). New requirement `product_truth`: `food-still-life` and `product-hero-video` will not make until an owner-confirmed reference of the real product exists (`library: own_asset`, approval locked or current); research images that seem to show the product stay inferred. creative-review reads the brand's corpus before any lens; brand-import keeps research positioning as inferred until the owner confirms; create-brand tells the founder where to drop their pictures and uses the reference board.
- **A reference board the founder can click** (field test F65). `cstack sheet board references/ --out work/sheets/refs.html` shows each reference with its image, mechanism and source link, and *Keep* or *Kill* with a line on why; `sheet import` writes `gold` and `anti` on the reference files, which is what the make gate counts. A board stands in for a written reference packet.
- **Phase budgets, waivers with a voice, and the text check named up front** (field test F64, F66, F67). Flow steps take a `budget`; `flows check` refuses one below its own unit cost, and the make gate warns when the phases add up to more than the run budget (grid-pick-polish carries explore 0.30, rebuild 0.35, edits 0.40). Waivers take `by` and `quote`, and the gate's warning carries the owner's words. When a stop rule runs `cstack image text` and tesseract is not on PATH, the make gate says so before the batch starts.
- **Grid → Pick → Polish** (owner's method note; field test F62, F63). New flow `grid-pick-polish`: one anchor, one grid image of 4 or 9 frames whose prompt says what stays fixed and what varies, the owner picks one to three winners and says why, each winner is rebuilt alone, edited one change at a time, and only finalists are upscaled; film expands from the winner with motion-only prompts. `cstack sheet make --grid 3x3` numbers each frame so single frames can be picked; the sheet now opens on winner picking (up to three, with reasons) and keeps pairs as the optional calibration step; `sheet import` records winners as approvals with `reason_codes` and the frame's `region`. `cstack sheet open` opens a sheet, and every sheet command prints its `file://` link and the steps, because the owner could not tell where to pick. Prompt templates in `skills/prompt-director/references/grid-pick-polish.md`.
- **The best model now, not a habit** (owner, field test F54 to F58, F60). `cstack route` ranks by measured quality first: image entries carry a dated leaderboard rank (`quality: {leaderboard, rank, as_of, source}`), and a rank counts while it is under 30 days old; tier, then price, break ties after it. The route always prints `best now`, the best-ranked model for the job, even when it cannot be called here, with what it needs (a provider, an adapter, a key, a price). A rank older than 30 days, or none for the top candidates, prints a warning naming the date and asks for a check of the live leaderboard before a paid batch; `cstack generate` says the same before an image run when the data is old. Only three ranks are recorded, as read on 2026-10-05: GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst #1, GPT Image 2 #3, FLUX.2 pro #41. fal serves GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (`openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image`), so with `FAL_KEY` it is the reachable #1; the Flare variant and the edit endpoint are listed on fal with ids not yet verified, so they have no fal route. Routes can carry per-token prices (`per: token`), estimated only from a request's `token_estimate` and otherwise unpriced with the reason. fal's GPT Image 2 route is priced per quality at 1024x1024 (low 0.006, medium 0.053, high 0.211 USD); other sizes stay unpriced. The `openai-image` adapter is now real: the Images API, generations as JSON and edits as multipart, with the key sent only to `https://api.openai.com` and scrubbed from errors, calls that never left marked not charged, and routes for GPT Image 2 and both 2.5 variants, unpriced until a per-token price is verified. `notSent`/`neverSent` moved to `providers/errors.mjs`.
- **A reaction to the whole board is not a reaction to references** (field test F36). The make gate needs reactions to at least two individual references since the last pivot; a reaction to the reference packet or a folder no longer counts.
- **Keys from a .env without a shell** (field test F47). `--env-from <file>` loads only the provider variables cstack declares, never prints a value, and reports malformed lines by number. The preamble says never to cat, grep or source a `.env`.
- **Calls that never left this machine cost nothing** (field test F46). A fal request that fails on DNS, a refused connection, TLS or a malformed key header is booked as not charged, like a 4xx; a reset after sending stays booked.

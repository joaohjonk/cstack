# Owner interview, 2026-09-14 (fictional)

Interviewer: /brief run. Interviewee: the studio founder (a fictional person; referred to as "Founder").
Everything below is invented for the cstack example workspace.

1. "We make stoneware tableware in small batches: plates, bowls, cups. Nothing else. No vases, no decor."
2. "A batch is one kiln load. Usually 60 to 90 pieces. When a glaze run is gone, it is gone."
3. "Every piece is wheel-thrown or slab-built in the studio, then glazed by hand. Glaze pools and speckles differ piece to piece, and that is the point."
4. "Never call it handcrafted luxury. Never say artisanal. We are a workshop, not a boutique."
5. "Our voice is plain and specific. Tell people what the clay is and how hot the kiln got."
6. "Photography should look like the table after people have eaten, not before. Real crumbs are fine; fake steam is not."
7. "I am not sure who buys most. My guess is people furnishing a first proper home, but I have no data."
8. "Do not claim microwave safe until the supplier sends test results. Dishwasher safe is fine, it is on the care card."
9. "The ochre on the swing tag and the ochre on the website do not match and it annoys me. I have not decided which one is right."

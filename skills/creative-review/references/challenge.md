# The challenge: founder-first, not founder-only

A review that only scores the owner's direction against the owner's taste can at best match them; the field test
showed exactly that (F87: reviewers scored at or below the founder and never proposed anything stronger). Every
review, and every create-brand checkpoint, ends with a challenge memo the owner answers.

Write `work/direction/<date>-challenge.md` (or `trial/challenge.md` in a trial):

1. **The safe choice.** One line naming what the current direction plays safe, and the evidence (a category map row,
   a shelf capture, a competitor's ad, the owner's own references).
2. **The braver version.** One stronger idea inside the brand's beliefs, not a new style: what it is, the one
   application it would change first, and what it risks. Mechanisms from the canon, never a named practitioner's look.
3. **Price.** Is the price position argued, or assumed? The question a buyer or a retail buyer would ask.
4. **Claim.** What the brand says it is, and what proves it; a claim with no proof is a question for claims-proof.
5. **Positioning.** Who it is not for, and whether the work makes that visible.
6. **What would change my mind.** The evidence that would make the safe choice right.

The owner keeps, adopts or merges; the answer and their words go in `state/feedback.jsonl` (`comment` events). The
memo never blocks: it is there so the owner decides against something better than their first idea, not alone.

# Where claim rules live, by jurisdiction

Starting points for step 4 of [claims-proof](../SKILL.md). Always retrieve the current primary text; record citation, version or date, and retrieval date. Not legal advice: high-risk items go to counsel.

## Brazil
- ANVISA: the applicable RDC and IN for the category (labeling, nutrition, allergens, mandatory statements), in Portuguese.
- CONAR's self-regulation code for advertising; the Consumer Defence Code (CDC, Lei 8.078/1990) on misleading and abusive advertising.

## United States
- FDA: the applicable CFR parts and guidance (Nutrition or Supplement Facts, identity, net quantity, ingredients, allergens, manufacturer or distributor statement).
- FTC Act section 5 (substantiation), the Endorsement Guides, and the rule on fake reviews and testimonials.

## European Union
- Regulation (EC) 1924/2006 on nutrition and health claims, and the EU register of authorised claims.
- Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 on food information to consumers.
- Directive 2006/114/EC on comparative advertising: verifiable, like-for-like, no denigration, no riding on a competitor's mark.
- The Unfair Commercial Practices Directive as amended by Directive (EU) 2024/825 (generic environmental claims, sustainability labels).

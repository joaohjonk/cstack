# Ads lens

what marketers and creators are doing, as distinct from `cultural-scan`'s what is happening. Sources: Foreplay's official MCP or API (running duration, copy, CTA, landing page), Meta Ad Library API (commercial ads only where they reached the EU), TikTok Creative Center by a person's browser; never scraped. Tag each ad with the taxonomy families (`registry/creative-taxonomy.json`) and record, dated:
- **Direct competitors**: what they repeat, what they stopped, which concepts run longest (duration is a weak proxy for performance, never proof).
- **Adjacent winners**: brands outside the category whose mechanisms could transfer.
- **Creator and platform world**: native formats and mechanics becoming common.
- **Saturation**: the angle, format or claim everyone copies.
- **White space**: a mechanism that works elsewhere and has not entered the category.
Output mechanisms, not ads to remake: "long-running in the category: transformation mechanic in before-and-after format, 7 of 10 brands", never "make their ad".

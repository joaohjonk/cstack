# Philosophy

cstack encodes a point of view about creative work with AI. These are the beliefs behind the design, each with the rule it turns into. A belief without an operational rule does not belong here. The thinkers and practitioners these beliefs come from, and what each one contributed, are in [research/taste-influences.md](research/taste-influences.md).

## Taste is the objective function

AI widens what can be made; it does not decide what is good. Taste decides, and in a brand that taste belongs to the owner. **Rule:** owner picks, kills and edits are recorded as preference data and outrank any AI judge. No skill may present a model's opinion as the owner's.

## Associative distance is the search radius

Work that only looks at its own category converges on the category average. Distinctive work borrows mechanisms from far away and lands them precisely. **Rule:** reference searches plan a near / middle / far mix before searching; ten references from one field is a failure (`reference_monoculture`).

## Collect models, not looks

A reference is useful for its mechanism, not its surface. **Rule:** every kept reference records *Source → Mechanism → Transfer* and a do-not-copy note, and passes the removal test: delete the image, and the mechanism should still explain itself. Names of people and studios never enter a generation prompt; mechanisms do.

## Observe, compare, articulate, decide, encode

Taste compounds only when it is written down at the right level. **Rule:** as evidence repeats, a learning is encoded at the lowest level that holds it reliably, following the promotion ladder in [learnings.md](learnings.md#promotion-targets).

## Knowledge has a status

A fact from a founder's mouth, a pattern scraped from a website and a model's guess are not the same kind of thing. **Rule:** every brand field carries a status (locked, current, testing, provisional, inferred, conflict, unknown, deprecated, killed, historical) and a source. Killed and deprecated rules stay recorded so they are not reintroduced (`retired_rule_resurfaced`).

## A gap is an answer

A plausible invented fact is worse than an admitted unknown, because it gets built on. **Rule:** UNKNOWN is a valid output; inferred values are labelled until confirmed.

## Culture is observed, not assumed

Model memory is not current culture. **Rule:** cultural signals need a source, a date and a stage; brands borrow from a community only with a stated permission; ideas are scored as ownable assets on legibility, repetition, mobility, aggregation and organization, never summed into one number.

## The canon is a lattice of mental models

Great practitioners matter for how they thought, not for their look. **Rule:** canon entries state the mental model, the mechanisms, when it helps, when it does not, and its tensions with other entries. Canon is a lens, never a template (`canon_ossification`).

## Judgments are plural

Beautiful, on brand, culturally alive, clear, well crafted, true to the product, commercially useful and correct are different questions. **Rule:** reviews keep the axes separate, judge against a stated baseline, show disagreement, and let hard gates block regardless of any composite.

## Nobody certifies their own work

**Rule:** the generator never verifies its own output. Deterministic checks first, then an independent reviewer, then the owner when stakes are meaningful (`self_certified` is a recorded failure).

## Deterministic before generative, cheap before premium

**Rule:** reuse → retrieve → deterministic transform → cheap probe → premium generation. Real marks and type are composited from official files, not approximated. Spend is estimated, capped and stopped by a condition before it starts.

## Prior work is capital

**Rule:** before searching, prompting or generating, look for an answer that already exists in the workspace's ledgers, lineage and approved artifacts.

## Brand-agnostic by construction

The tool is shared; brands are not. **Rule:** cstack contains no brand's data. Each brand's state lives in its own repository, and only anonymized, general mechanisms flow back into cstack.
